Our Story

Riverside Bake & Brew started in 2020 as a small home bakery in Vereeniging, selling fresh bread and pastries to neighbours. Word of mouth grew the customer base quickly, and by 2021 we opened our first shopfront along the Vaal River, adding a coffee counter alongside the bakery.

Our Mission

Riverside Bake & Brew exists to bring honest, handmade bread, pastries and coffee to the Vaal community, made fresh daily using quality local ingredients, and to make it simple for customers to order ahead or have their favourites delivered.

Our Vision

To grow from a single riverside shopfront into the most trusted name in artisan baking and coffee in the Vaal region, known for quality, consistency, and a genuine connection to the local community.

Our Ingredients

  • Locally milled flour from Gauteng suppliers
  • Free-range eggs and dairy from nearby farms
  • Coffee beans roasted in small batches by a local roastery
  • No preservatives or artificial additives
